References & Method Citations

[1]
From Demodulation to Decoding: Toward Complete LoRa PHY Understanding and Implementation (Dual-Band Dechirping)
Zhenqiang Xu, Shuai Tong, Pengjin Xie, Jiliang Wang
ACM Transactions on Sensor Networks (TOSN) • https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3546869

Demodulation method: Estimate Symbol without Downsampling (Dual-Energy Sum). In oversampled discrete-time dechirping, chirp frequencies wrap across \([-BW/2, +BW/2]\), dividing symbol energy into positive and negative frequency components. Summing peak energy across both conjugate bands avoids boundary loss and achieves complete energy recovery.

[2]
NELoRa: Towards Ultra-low SNR LoRa Communication with Neural-enhanced Demodulation
Chenning Li, Hanqing Guo, Shuai Tong, Xiao-Yang Liu, Jiliang Wang
ACM SenSys 2021 • https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3485730.3485928

Demodulation method: NeLoRa. Employs a dilated convolutional neural network (maskCNN) to learn spectrogram-level noise patterns and denoise undechirped STFT representations, paired with a hybrid CNN-LSTM classifier that directly infers transmitted symbol indices at deep negative SNRs.

[3]
LoRaSeek: Boosting Denoising Ability in Neural-enhanced LoRa Decoder via Hierarchical Feature Extraction
ACM MobiCom 2025
ACM MobiCom • https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3680207.3765241

Demodulation method: LoRaSeek. Uses STFT Transformer and dual-attention U-Net architectures to extract multi-scale hierarchical time-frequency features, performing waveform reconstruction via Nyquist ISTFT followed by conventional dechirp-FFT peak detection.

[4]
GLoRiPHY: Enhancing LoRa Reception with Generative Models: Channel-Aware Denoising of LoRaPHY Signals
ACM SenSys 2024
ACM SenSys • https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3666025.3699354

Demodulation method: GLoRiPHY. Introduces channel-aware generative Conformer architectures combining convolutional layers and multi-head self-attention to recover corrupted LoRa chirp signals under harsh fading and extreme negative SNR conditions.

[5]
CALoRa: Content-Aware Preamble Detection and Waveform Restoration for LoRa Networks
IEEE
IEEE • https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11386915

Preamble detection & restoration method: CALoRa. Uses deep convolutional neural networks for robust preamble location under severe CFO/STO offsets and reconstructs damaged time-domain chirp waveforms prior to decoding.
